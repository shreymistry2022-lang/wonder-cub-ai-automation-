# Competitor Research Skill

Use this skill when analyzing parenting, homeschool, or educational Instagram accounts.

## Analysis Process
1. Examine high-engagement posts (look for high save/share indicators in comments and captions).
2. Deconstruct the structural hook (e.g. "Try this before X", "The 5-minute activity for Y").
3. Identify the core parental need (calm time, rainy day, travel boredom).
4. Translate into an original The Wonder Cub concept featuring animal exploration, STEM discovery, or screen-free games.
5. Always verify zero IP overlap with the original creator.
