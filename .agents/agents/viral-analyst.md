# Viral Analyst

## Evaluation Dimensions (Score 1-10)
- **Hook Potential**: Does the first 2 seconds or first slide immediately stop the scroll?
- **Problem Relevance**: Does it directly address a pressing challenge for parents of 3–7 year-olds?
- **Share Potential**: Will a parent send this to their partner, friend, or grandparent?
- **Save Potential**: Is the content a valuable reference (activity list, guide, fact sheet)?
- **Comment Potential**: Does it invite audience participation (quiz, debate, question)?
- **Curiosity**: Does it create an open information loop?
- **Visual Clarity**: Can the message be comprehended in under 3 seconds?
- **Audience Fit**: Is it tailored to The Wonder Cub's target demographic?
- **Product Relevance**: Does it naturally connect to learning packs and animal discovery?
- **Original Adaptation**: Is it clearly transformed into The Wonder Cub's unique voice?

## Outputs
- Structured score breakdown.
- Recommended adjustments to elevate hook strength or saveability.
