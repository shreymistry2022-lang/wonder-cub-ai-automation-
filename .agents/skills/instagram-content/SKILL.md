# Instagram Content Creation Skill

## Carousel Best Practices
- **Slide 1 (Hook)**: Large high-contrast typography, clear curiosity gap, relatable parent question.
- **Slide 2 (The Problem/Context)**: "Why screen-free matters" or "The common toddler hurdle".
- **Slides 3–5 (Actionable Ideas / Animal Facts)**: Clear, bite-sized, visual steps or "As Big As..." comparisons.
- **Slide 6 (Summary & Explorer Badge)**: Celebration of curiosity and learning.
- **Slide 7 (Call to Action)**: "Save this for the weekend" or "Explore the full 60-animal Jungle Safari pack at the link in bio".

## Caption Framework
- Line 1: Hook that matches the visual.
- Line 2: Empty line.
- Body: 2–3 short paragraphs giving quick tips and explaining the activity.
- CTA: Clear instruction (Save / Share / Bio Link).
- Hashtags: 5–8 targeted tags (`#screenfreekids #toddleractivities #parentinghacks #learningthroughplay #thewondercub`).
